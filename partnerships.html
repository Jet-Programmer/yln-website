Young Leaders Network is building relationships with universities, organisations and communities that want young people to learn, contribute and lead.

Why partner with YLN?

Connect your work with emerging talent and fresh ideas.

Partnerships can help young people find a pathway into learning and work, while giving organisations a meaningful way to listen, collaborate and invest in future leaders.

Built together

We want collaborations to be shaped with young people and grounded in clear goals, useful experiences and respectful relationships.
