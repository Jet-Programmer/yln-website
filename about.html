Who we are

A network built around young people and possibility.

Young Leaders Network is a growing community connecting young people with opportunities, learning and relationships that help them make a difference.

Our story

Leadership starts with a chance to contribute.

YLN is being created as a bridge between young people and the organisations, experiences and ideas that can help them thrive. We are at the start of that journey, and the network will grow through the voices and contributions of its founding community.

Our purpose

To connect young people to opportunities, knowledge and one another, so they can grow their confidence and shape positive change in their communities.

Mission

Connect. Equip. Empower.

Build pathways that help young people access opportunities, develop skills and participate in meaningful leadership.

Vision

A future shaped by young leaders.

A connected world where young people are equipped to contribute, influence and lead in the places they call home.

Our principles

How we want to work.

Access

Make pathways and information easier to discover.

Growth

Support learning, confidence and practical experience.

Connection

Bring people and organisations together with purpose.

Agency

Make space for young people to shape the work.
