Featured project · Climate innovation

AI4Climate: smarter irrigation decisions.

A climate-smart agriculture prototype brings together soil, crop and weather signals to explore irrigation decision support. It includes historical research on hourly soil-moisture forecasting; its dashboard examples are illustrative, and the model is not a live farm prediction.
